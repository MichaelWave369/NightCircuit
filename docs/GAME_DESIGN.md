# Game Design Contract

## Working title

**Φ: Night Circuit**

## Premise

A Hunter and a damaged AI companion move through a world left unstable by the Night Wars. Towns, ruins, and inhabitants disagree about what exists and what happened. The disagreement is not merely narrative: some contradictions correspond to real differences in world state.

## Player fantasy

The human player becomes an increasingly capable explorer and fighter.

Φ-Bot becomes an increasingly capable co-player whose perception, mobility, and reasoning are different from the Hunter's.

Neither side is intended to become an omniscient hint system.

## Pillars

### 1. Deliberate exploration

Rooms must reward observation, memory, and revisiting old spaces after capability changes.

### 2. Learnable combat

Bosses teach attack grammar. Strong encounters may later violate or bifurcate that grammar, but readable rules come first.

### 3. Towns matter

NPC schedules, stores, access routes, rumors, and danger change across the day/night cycle.

### 4. Evidence is progression

Observations enter the Reality Ledger. Contradictions can reveal routes, challenge testimony, or unlock investigations.

### 5. Φ-Bot is embodied

Φ-Bot occupies space, has energy and forms, can fail, can be separated from the Hunter, and can perform authorized actions.

### 6. Asymmetric knowledge

The Hunter may see physical cues that Φ-Bot misses. Φ-Bot may detect signals the Hunter cannot directly perceive.

### 7. Altermath is mechanical

Altermath is the working name for globally similar or apparently neutral states whose internal causal structure differs in a way that matters. In the game, this becomes traversal, combat, puzzle, and world-state machinery rather than exposition alone.

## Early Φ-Bot forms

1. **Broken**: Light, Inspect, Follow, Hold
2. **Scout**: Resonance Ping, Anchor Mark, Enemy Read, Contradiction Sense
3. **Echo**: reconstruct partial event traces
4. **Gear**: interact with nested mechanisms and timed structures
5. **Bubble**: stabilize or traverse bounded alternate states
6. **Altermath**: perceive and manipulate causally distinct but superficially equivalent states

Names after Scout remain design targets, not promises of final content.
