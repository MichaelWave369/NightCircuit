## Qualification boundary

NC-015 qualifies the architecture and progression loop. It does not claim final art, final tuning, broad hardware certification, or shipping polish.

## First-hour promise

The player should understand within the first hour that:

1. the map remembers what they noticed;
2. Φ-Bot sees things they do not;
3. NPC testimony can conflict with observed reality;
4. a boss can require cooperation between viewpoints;
5. a new ability changes old spaces immediately.
